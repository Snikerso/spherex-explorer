## Deadline and requirements

Based on the [Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/#project-submission-start-end-times), published 13 November and read 14 November 2026:

- Submission window: **14 November 09:00 to 15 November 23:59**, in the team's Local Event timezone. For Universal Event, use the Team Owner's residential timezone. If applicable, Europe/Warsaw uses CET. Local judging may have an earlier deadline.
- Form content and linked materials must be English. Non-English video speech requires English subtitles.
- Required demo: **video ≤30 seconds or a deck ≤7 slides**, including title/intro slides. Exceeding the limit prevents Global Judging eligibility.
- Host demo/project externally without login or permission requests. The page accepts JPG/PNG/GIF images up to 10 MB, not document/video uploads.
- Global Awards require at least one NASA resource. Participants must be confirmed at the same Local Event. For a solo entry, the owner's registration must still match the team's event.
- Disclose SI use. Generated images/video require a visible label; audio needs descriptive/metadata acknowledgement; code/data need descriptive/metadata acknowledgement. Generated content may not contain or modify NASA branding/design elements.
- Demo/final project may not include likenesses or voices of people under 18.
- Evaluation considers Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance and Presentation. The form description remains essential.

## Website steps

1. Open Profile → My Team → Project → Edit. Save progress regularly and use the edit lock when appropriate.
2. Paste completed English form fields, actual hosted demo URL and public project URL.
3. Test links in a signed-out/private browser. Check readability, playback, language and absence of secrets/private session history.
4. After checking authorship and requirements, personally review the Terms and Conditions and originality declarations.
5. Click **Submit for Judging** before the deadline. Save Progress alone does not submit.
6. Verify the success banner/confirmation and keep evidence. Resubmit after later changes before the window closes.
